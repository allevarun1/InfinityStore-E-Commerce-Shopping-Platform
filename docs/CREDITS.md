# Photo and video credits (demo catalogue)

The ten extra sample sarees (and the reels made from them) use openly licensed photographs from
Wikimedia Commons. They are demo content for a local or staging store. **Replace them with your own
photography before you launch.**

Reels are adaptations (crops, zooms, grading, captions) of these photos, so the same licence applies to them.
CC BY-SA requires credit and that adaptations are shared under the same licence; CC BY requires credit;
CC0 and public domain need neither. The photo licence does not cover the personality rights of people shown.
The nine first sample sarees and their reels predate this file; confirm their source before launch too.
